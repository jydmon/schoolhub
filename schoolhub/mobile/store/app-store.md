## Description
SIPlat brings a school's whole community into one secure app. Everyone sees
exactly what's relevant to them — nothing more.

Parents / guardians
- Dashboards for every child across every school
- Attendance, behaviour, homework, reports and school trips
- Calendar, timetable and daily menu
- Real-time school-bus tracking and pick-up/drop-off updates
- Notifications and secure messaging with the school

Teachers
- Assigned pupils, timetable and calendar
- Take attendance, log behaviour and write pupil reports
- Manage trips and see the day at a glance
- An AI assistant scoped strictly to your own pupils

Drivers
- Today's journeys, start/stop routes and check pupils in and out
- Share live location only while a route is running
- Pre-trip vehicle checks and messages from the transport office

School & transport staff
- Manage pupils, staff, routes, fleet and communications
- Live transport control centre and reporting

Security & privacy first
- Access is strictly role-based and limited to your own school
- Parents only ever see their own children
- Location is shared only during a live journey, never afterwards
