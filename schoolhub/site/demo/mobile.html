Free to download · access by invitation only
Downloading the app never grants access. Only users invited by a subscribing school can activate it. Parents can't self-register — the school (or its MIS) identifies authorised contacts and sends an invite.
ONBOARDING
  1. Download from the App Store or Google Play
  2. Open the app & follow your school's invitation link
  3. Verify your email & enter the activation code
  4. Create a password or use Single Sign-On
  5. Accept the latest Terms & Privacy Policy
  6. Complete MFA if your school requires it
  7. Your account links to your school & role — see your dashboard
The app automatically shows your school's branding (logo, colours, welcome message and contacts). Access is secured by RBAC, tenant isolation and parent–child verification.
