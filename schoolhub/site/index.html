For teachers, leaders & trusts

Connect what you have — don't replace it.

SIPlat sits above your MIS and tools, syncs them in, and gives leaders one dashboard and families one app. Teachers keep the systems they know.

  • ✓Keep your MIS & tools. Connect by API, secure import or migration — no double entry, clear source of truth.
  • ✓Announcements to all or some. Message parents by app, email, WhatsApp or SMS, honouring consent.

  • ✓Safe by default. Tenant isolation, role‑based access, pupil‑data masking and full audit.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do we have to replace our MIS?

No. SIPlat connects to your existing MIS and tools and keeps them as the source of truth — there's no rip‑and‑replace.

Is our data secure?

Yes — tenant isolation, encryption at rest and in transit, pupil‑data masking, role‑based access and full audit. UK GDPR throughout.

Can a parent with children in different schools see them all?

Yes — as long as each school uses SIPlat, a parent sees all of their children in one app.

How long does onboarding take?

Most schools go live within 2–3 weeks, onboarded in controlled waves.

What does it cost?

We'll share full pricing at launch. Parents get SIPlat free; there'll be an optional premium upgrade and school/trust plans. Register your interest and we'll send you the details — and an early look — as soon as they're ready.
